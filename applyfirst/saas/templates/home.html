Be first to apply on onlinejobs.ph

ApplyFirst watches the jobs you care about and writes a ready-to-paste application the moment a match appears — delivered straight to your own Gmail inbox. You review, copy, and paste it yourself. We don't apply for you; we make sure you're first.

Continue with Google

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google and connect Gmail.
  2. Add your job keywords and a standard message.
  3. When a matching job is posted, we tailor an application and email it to your inbox.
  4. You open it, review, and apply.

Why we ask for Gmail access

ApplyFirst requests the gmail.send permission for one purpose: to send you your own tailored application, to your own inbox, so it's ready when you apply. We never read, list, or store your existing emails — only the send permission is requested. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect and how it's used.
